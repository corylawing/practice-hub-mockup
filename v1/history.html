Look through the history

Every saved version of one of the hub's records, straight from SharePoint — further back than the panel in SharePoint will scroll. It only ever writes if you press Put this back on a version and confirm it.

What to look for

Pick the record and type a word to hunt for. Versions containing it are highlighted, and for the doctor list the names are shown in order — the calendar stores doctors by position, so the order is what matters.
