Production in Home-Brace

Home-Brace can now keep the production numbers itself, so the Excel workbook won’t be needed any more. Every step is below, with a picture of the screen. The pictures use made-up numbers.

Once · you · when you’re ready

1 · Moving off the Excel

Nothing changes until you press the switch. Until then, everything works exactly as it does today.

  1. For now, keep using your Excel. Each time you save it, Home-Brace makes its own copy and checks that every number comes out the same.
  2. Plan 2027 in Home-Brace. 2027 is already set up - you just go office by office (see Plan next year).
  3. When you feel ready - before January: go to Admin → Production data. In the box Where the production numbers live, press Switch to Home-Brace…
  4. Check, then switch. All three lines should have a ✓. Leave Keep Heather’s Excel up to date too ticked. Press Switch now.
  5. That’s it. The offices type their numbers in Enter Production the same way as today. The dashboard now shows Home-Brace’s numbers, and your Excel still gets every number.
  6. Cut the connection when you no longer need the Excel. In the same box, press Stop updating her Excel. Your Excel gets nothing new from then on, and Home-Brace keeps everything. You can still get an Excel copy any time (see Get the Excel).

Changed your mind? Every step can be undone in the same box: Keep her Excel up to date again reconnects it, and Switch back to her Excel… puts everything back the way it was.

Every month · the offices

2 · Enter the month’s numbers

In Enter Production. This is the same before and after the switch.

  1. Pick the office and the month.
  2. Type the month’s numbers: TC Net Production, Medicaid starts, write-offs and completed production days. Everything else is worked out, and the right side shows the result as you type.
  3. Press Save. The dashboard shows it right away.

In January: December’s numbers belong to last year. Choose last year in the Year box first, then December.

Once a year · you · usually in December

3 · Plan next year

In Admin → Production data → Plan the year.

  1. Set up the year - one click. Choose next year and press Set up 2027. Home-Brace copies this year’s set-up, and this year’s numbers become “last year”. The offices don’t see any change until January.
  2. Plan each office. Click an office at the top and answer three things:
    • Grow over last year by a % (offices with Medicaid also have a Medicaid %).
    • The stretch goal is the goal + a %.
    • Case fees: the average case fee (and the Medicaid case fee).
  3. Fill in the production days, then save. Type each month’s days, or press Use last year’s production days. Then press Save Carlsbad’s plan and Next: … for the next office. A ✓ shows next to each office that’s done.

A month that’s different? Click Different growth for some months and type that month’s % - or a dollar goal like $40,000. A month left empty uses the usual %.

Change your mind any time: open Plan the year, change it, save again. Every save shows Undo, and older saves are in History. Changing one year never changes another.

Rarely · you · when the plan doesn’t cover it

4 · Change how something is worked out

In Admin → Production data → Advanced → Show → Formulas.

Why it’s there: so you can change any calculation yourself, for any year, without Excel or anyone’s help. Each year keeps its own formulas, and the Excel download carries them.

When you’d use it: Plan the year already covers growth, the stretch goal, case fees and production days. Use Formulas only when a row should be worked out a different way - for example:

  • a row shows #REF! and needs to point at the right rows again;
  • an office’s goal should come from its stretch goal for a few months (the stretch goal × 0.7);
  • a goal should have a fixed amount added for a few months, say for a new doctor (like the picture below).
  1. Open Advanced. At the bottom of Production data, press Show.
  2. Find the row. In Formulas, pick the Year and the office’s Tab, then type part of the row’s name in Find a row… Each row says, in words, how it’s worked out. Press Change on the one you want.
  3. Pick the months, write the new way, check it, save. Choose the months (or All). Write how it’s worked out - add a row with Add a row and the + - × ÷ buttons; numbers like 1.05 or 5% work too. Before you save, each month shows Now and New, and Also changes lists anything else that follows. Then press Save.

While a year is still copied from your Excel (2026 right now), your next Excel save puts your Excel’s formulas back - so change formulas in 2027, or after the switch.

Whenever you need it

5 · Get the Excel

In Admin → Production data → Years kept.

Press ⬇ Download Excel next to the year. You get that year’s workbook, laid out like yours, with all its formulas. History shows every change - who made it and when - with Undo.

Good to know

Can I plan next year before this year is over?
Yes. Numbers entered later for this year (like December, typed in January) are carried into next year’s “last year” by themselves.
What happens to old years?
They stay, each year on its own. Plan the year only shows this year and next year. Older years are under Years kept, ready to download.
Who can change the plan?
Admins. Office managers enter their own office’s numbers in Enter Production.
What else is under Advanced?
Goals, box by box: one office’s goal for one month, as a growth number (1.05 = last year + 5%) or a dollar amount. Plan the year does the same more simply.
Something looks wrong?
Check History first - it shows every change, who made it and when, with Undo. Or tell us with the 👋 Tour & Feedback button at the bottom right of any page.
