Production in Home-Brace

Home-Brace can now keep the production numbers itself, so the Excel workbook won’t be needed any more. The offices type their numbers each month, and you plan the goals once a year.

Starting now · you · at your own pace

Moving off the Excel

Nothing changes until you press the switch. Until then, everything works exactly as it does today.

  1. For now, keep using your Excel. Each time you save it, Home-Brace makes its own copy and checks that every number comes out the same. You can see it in Admin → Production data: a green ✓ means they match.
  2. Plan 2027 in Home-Brace. 2027 is already set up - go office by office in Plan the year (explained below).
  3. When you feel ready - before January: in Admin → Production data, under Where the production numbers live, press Switch to Home-Brace…, check that all three lines have a ✓, leave Keep Heather’s Excel up to date too ticked, and press Switch now. The offices type their numbers in Enter Production the same way as today; the dashboard now shows Home-Brace’s numbers, and your Excel still gets every number.
  4. Cut the connection when you no longer need the Excel. Same place: press Stop updating her Excel. From then on your Excel gets nothing new and Home-Brace keeps everything. You can still get an Excel copy any time with ⬇ Download Excel under Years kept.

Changed your mind? Every step can be undone in the same place: Keep her Excel up to date again reconnects it, and Switch back to her Excel… puts everything back the way it was.

Every month · the offices

Enter the month’s numbers

In Enter Production.

  1. Pick the office and the month.
  2. Type the month’s numbers: TC Net Production, Medicaid starts, write-offs and completed production days.
  3. Press Save. The dashboard shows it right away.

In January: December’s numbers belong to last year. Choose last year in the Year box first, then December.

Once a year · you · usually in December

Plan next year

In Admin → Production data → Plan the year.

  1. Set up the year. Choose next year and press Set up 2027. Home-Brace copies this year’s set-up, and this year’s numbers become “last year”. The offices don’t see any change until January.
  2. Plan each office. Click an office and answer three things:
    • Grow over last year by 4 % - offices with Medicaid also have a Medicaid %.
    • The stretch goal is the goal + 10 %.
    • Case fees - the average case fee (and the Medicaid case fee).
    Then the production days for each month. The grey numbers are last year’s; Use last year’s production days fills them in.

  3. Press Save Carlsbad’s plan, then Next: Clovis →. A ✓ shows next to each office that’s done.

A month that’s different? Click Different growth for some months and type that month’s % - or a dollar goal like $40,000. Leave a month empty and it uses the usual %.

Any time

Change your mind

  • Open Plan the year, change what you need, and save again. Plans can be changed all year.
  • Every save shows Undo. Older saves: Years kept → History → Undo this change.
  • Changing one year never changes another.

Rarely · you · when the plan doesn’t cover it

Change how something is worked out

In Admin → Production data → Advanced → Show → Formulas.

Why it’s there: so you can change any calculation yourself, for any year, without Excel or anyone’s help. Each year keeps its own formulas, and the Excel download carries them.

When you’d use it: Plan the year already covers growth, the stretch goal, case fees and production days. Use Formulas only when a row should be worked out a different way - for example:

  • a row shows #REF! and needs to point at the right rows again;
  • an office’s goal should come from its stretch goal for a few months (the stretch goal × 0.7);
  • a new number you want to follow, like production per day (production ÷ production days).
  1. Find the row. Pick the Year and the office’s Tab, then type part of the row’s name in Find a row…
  2. Press Change next to the months you want.
  3. Pick the months (or All), then write how it’s worked out: add a row with Add a row - type part of its name and pick it - and the + - × ÷ buttons. Numbers like 1.05 or 5% work too.
    2027 Goal · Jun–Sep · how it’s worked out
    Stretch Goal × 0.7
    Before you save, every month shows
    Now $52,000 → New $48,300
  4. Check before you save. Each month shows Now and New, and Also changes lists anything else that follows from it.
  5. Press Save · Jun–Sep 2027. It goes in History, with Undo.

Reading a formula: a row on its own means that row in the same month · a row with a month (like “Average Case Fee · Jan”) is that one box.

While a year is still copied from your Excel (2026 right now), your next Excel save puts your Excel’s formulas back - so change formulas in 2027, or after the switch.

Whenever you need it

The Excel

In Admin → Production data → Years kept, press ⬇ Download Excel next to the year. You get that year’s workbook, laid out like yours, with all its formulas.

Good to know

Can I plan next year before this year is over?
Yes. Numbers entered later for this year (like December, typed in January) are carried into next year’s “last year” by themselves.
What happens to old years?
They stay, each year on its own. Plan the year only shows this year and next year. Older years are under Years kept, ready to download.
Who can change the plan?
Admins. Office managers enter their own office’s numbers in Enter Production.
What’s under Advanced?
Goals, box by box: one office’s goal for one month, as a growth number (1.05 = last year + 5%) or a dollar amount. And Formulas: see Change how something is worked out, above.
Something looks wrong?
Check History first - it shows every change, who made it and when, with Undo. Or tell us with the 👋 Tour & Feedback button at the bottom right of any page.
