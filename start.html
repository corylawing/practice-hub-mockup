If the small window never appears

Your browser blocked it. Look at the right-hand end of the address bar at the top of the screen for a small icon, click it, and choose to always allow pop-ups from this site.

Then click Sign in with Microsoft again.

You will not see every tab
You see the ones for your role and your office. That is normal, not a fault.
Your name, top right
Your photo, phone number and “About me” — yours to change any time.
The bell, top right
Anything new for you. Click Got it on one and it will not come back.
If something looks out of date
Press F5 to refresh the page.

Keep it one tap away

Save Home-Brace to your phone or desktop and it opens with one tap, like any other app — no typing the address again.

iPhone / iPad
In Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom (a square with an arrow coming out of it), scroll down, and tap Add to Home Screen.
Android
In Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen.
Windows
In Edge, click the three dots at the top right, then Apps, then Install this site as an app. In Chrome it is the three dots, then Cast, save and share, then Install page as app.

Please fill in your profile

There are around 70 of us across eight offices, and most have never met. A photo and a line about what you do means the person calling from Lubbock knows who they are asking for.

Your name, top right → 👤 My profile
  • Photo. Drag a picture in, or choose one from your phone or computer. Any size — it is resized for you. No photo? Pick a colour for your initials instead.
  • Preferred name. What you would like to be called. This is the name your team sees.
  • Contact phone. Microsoft does not hold this one, so it is blank until you add it.
  • About me. One line on what you do.
All of this shows on the 👥 Team tab, where anyone can search by name, office, role or phone. Your name and email come from Microsoft, and your team and office are set by an admin — those four above are yours.
